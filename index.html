      Questions

      About the Farishta family dhikr app

      What is Farishta?

      Farishta is a family dhikr app. You create a room, share a short code, and everyone in the family taps + on their own phone. Every tap adds to one shared count that updates live on every phone until you reach your goal.

      How do I count dhikr together with my family?

      Open dhikr.farishta.si, enter your name, name the room, choose a dhikr and set a goal. Send the room code to your family group chat. Each person joins with their name and the code, and every tap counts toward the same total.

      Do I need to download an app or create an account?

      No. Farishta runs in the browser on any phone, tablet or computer. There is no download and no account, just a name and a room code.

      Which dhikr can we count?

      Farishta includes Subhan Allah, Alhamdulillah, Allahu Akbar, La ilaha illallah, Astaghfirullah, Subhanallahi wa bihamdihi, Subhanallahil Azeem, La hawla wa la quwwata illa billah, salawat on the Prophet ﷺ, Hasbunallahu wa ni'mal wakeel and Ya Salamu, each with Arabic, transliteration and meaning. You can also add your own dhikr or du'a.

      Can family members in different cities or countries count together?

      Yes. The count syncs live, so family anywhere in the world see the same number the moment anyone taps.

      Can I see my own count as well as the family total?

      Yes. Switch between Group and Personal to see the family total or your own taps. The room also lists how much each person has counted.

      What happens if we stop and come back later?

      Rooms keep their count. Leave and come back days later, and the room and its total are still there.

      Can I undo a mistaken tap?

      Yes. The − button takes back a tap so the count stays accurate.
